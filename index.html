Our Mission

We believe students shouldn't have to wait to make an impact.

VEXOVA creates opportunities for students to apply what they learn to real-world problems while helping nonprofits access the technology, research, and innovation they need to grow.

Research

Have research worth sharing?

VEXOVA provides a platform for students and emerging researchers to share research, findings, ideas, and perspectives surrounding technology, innovation, and social impact.

Submissions may be reviewed for publication, discussion, or inclusion in future VEXOVA research initiatives.

Submit Your Research

Submission takes about 5–10 minutes. You'll be asked for your name, school or organization, a short abstract, and either a link to your work or a file upload.

  • 1 Tell us about you and your research topic
  • 2 Share a short abstract and a link or file of your work
  • 3 Confirm originality and submit for review
